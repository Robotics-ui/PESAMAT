PesaMatrix Platform Capacity Enhancement

IMPORTANT

Do NOT rewrite or remove any existing functionality.

The current architecture already supports Distribution Masters.

The Platform Capacity widget is still showing a hardcoded capacity of 2,000.

This is incorrect.

The platform must calculate capacity dynamically from Distribution Masters.

====================================================
1. REMOVE HARDCODED CAPACITY
====================================================

Remove every hardcoded reference to:

2000

2500

10000

15000

or any fixed capacity.

Capacity must never be hardcoded.

====================================================
2. CALCULATE PLATFORM CAPACITY
====================================================

Read all ONLINE Distribution Masters.

Calculate:

Total Capacity =
SUM(distribution_masters.capacity)

Example:

Master A = 2500

Master B = 2500

Master C = 2500

Master D = 2500

Master E = 2500

Master F = 2500

Master G = 2500

Master H = 2500

Platform Capacity = 20,000

If later another master is added:

Platform Capacity automatically becomes 22,500.

No code changes should be required.

====================================================
3. ACTIVE ACCOUNTS
====================================================

Calculate

Active Accounts =
COUNT(active master_bindings)

This represents all subscribers currently assigned to Distribution Masters.

====================================================
4. REMAINING CAPACITY
====================================================

Remaining Capacity

=

Total Capacity

-

Active Accounts

====================================================
5. UTILIZATION
====================================================

Capacity Used %

=

(Active Accounts / Total Capacity) × 100

Show a progress bar.

Color coding:

0-50% Green

50-80% Yellow

80-95% Orange

95%+ Red

====================================================
6. PLATFORM LIMIT
====================================================

The platform is designed to support up to 50,000+ slave accounts.

Do NOT impose an artificial limit of 2,000.

Maximum supported capacity depends entirely on the number of ONLINE Distribution Masters.

Example:

8 Masters × 2,500 = 20,000

12 Masters × 2,500 = 30,000

20 Masters × 2,500 = 50,000

30 Masters × 2,500 = 75,000

The dashboard should automatically scale.
